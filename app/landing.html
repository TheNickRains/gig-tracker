Gig Collective

The booking pipeline for working musicians.
Open the app

Gig Collective is a personal booking assistant for independent and touring musicians. Track the venues and contacts you're working, run your outreach pipeline, draft and send booking emails, and keep your gig calendar in sync — so you can spend less time chasing and more time playing.

Venue & contact book

Capture every venue, booker, and business card in one organized place.

A pipeline that moves itself

Outreach, waiting, follow-up, won — your pipeline advances as the conversation does.

Email-aware outreach

Draft and send booking emails, and let replies update your pipeline automatically.

Calendar & availability

See your real availability and avoid double-booking when proposing dates.

How it uses your Google account
With your permission, Gig Collective connects to your Google account to send booking emails from your Gmail, read replies so your pipeline stays current, and sync your Google Calendar for availability. Your Google data is used only to provide these features — never sold, never used for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for the full details, including our Google API Limited Use commitment.
